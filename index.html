You get the deposit back if

  • You cancel or reschedule with 24 hours notice or more — refunded in full, no questions.
  • We reschedule for any reason, including weather. Always refunded in full.
  • We arrive, find something that changes the price, and you decide not to go ahead. You are never charged for a re-quote you didn't accept.
  • We can't reach the vehicle, or the site turns out to be unworkable through no fault of yours.

You lose the deposit only if

  • You cancel with less than 24 hours notice, or nobody is there when we arrive.
  • The vehicle is unreachable on the day — locked garage, blocked in, keys unavailable — and we can't get hold of you.

That's the whole point of it: a mobile slot is several hours plus travel, and a same-day cancellation can't be refilled. It's the smallest amount that makes a no-show hurt a little instead of not at all.

What could change the quoted price

  • Heavy pet hair, smoke odour, mould, or biological spills.
  • A vehicle materially different from what you described.
  • Existing damage that needs working around — we'll photograph it before we start.

In every one of those cases we stop and re-quote before touching the car. You approve the new number or you walk away with your deposit back. The price never moves after the work is done.

What we can't promise

  • Permanent defects: dye transfer, bleach spots, sun-cracked leather, etched paint, deep scratches through the clear coat.
  • Removal of odours bonded into foam or headliner — improvement, yes; elimination, not always.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do you need my water or electricity?

No. The van carries its own water tank and power, so we can work at an apartment complex or an office lot the same as a house. If a spigot is handy we'll happily use it, but nothing depends on it.

How much space do you need?

About 15 feet around the vehicle — roughly a standard driveway or two parking spaces. Covered parking is a bonus in bad weather but not required.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is usually fine for interior work. For exterior polishing or ceramic coating we'll reschedule, at no charge, to protect the result. We'll call you the morning of if the forecast turns.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Plenty of clients leave the keys and go to work. We'll send photos when we start and when we finish, and the walk-around can happen by video if you'd rather.

Why is it "starting at"?

A three-row SUV takes longer than a Civic, and a car with heavy pet hair or ground-in salt takes longer still. We quote the real number up front once we've seen photos, so the price you agree to is the price you pay.

You're new — why should I trust you with my car?

Fair question, and the honest answer is that you shouldn't take our word for it. So it's set up so you don't have to: the price is fixed before we start, the only thing taken up front is a small refundable deposit to hold the slot, and the balance isn't due until you've inspected the car with us. Cipher Auto Lab is a registered Connecticut LLC and fully insured — ask and we'll send the certificate before you book. If we damage something, it's covered and you'll hear it from us first.

Why is there a deposit, and can I get it back?

A mobile slot is several hours plus travel, and a same-day cancellation can't be refilled — the deposit is the smallest amount that makes that sting a little instead of not at all. It's $25 to $150 depending on the package, it comes straight off your total rather than being an extra charge, and you get it back in full if you give 24 hours notice, if we reschedule for weather, or if we turn up, find something that changes the price and you'd rather not go ahead. You only lose it for a same-day cancellation or a no-show.

What if a stain won't come out?

Some won't. Dye transfer, old bleach spots, sun-cracked leather and ground-in salt damage are sometimes permanent, and no amount of product changes that. If it's visible going in you'll be told before the quote, and if it turns up mid-job we stop and talk to you rather than quietly charge for a result we can't deliver.

When does window tinting start?

We're targeting later this year. Mention it when you book and we'll add you to the early list — that group gets first appointments and launch pricing.
