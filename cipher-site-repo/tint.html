Same look, far less heat. Ceramic blocks much more of the infrared that turns a parked car into an oven.

  • Everything carbon does

The law, plainly

Legal tint is the default here. If you want darker up front, I'll tell you what it costs you at a traffic stop before anything gets cut.

  • Front side windows: 35% or lighter
  • Behind the driver: darker is allowed, with both side mirrors on the car
  • Windshield: a strip above the AS-1 line only
  • The film maker's label goes on every window I tint
  • Medical exemption? Bring the paperwork and I'll work to it
In writing
What the warranty covers

The film carries a lifetime manufacturer warranty. Here's the honest shape of it.

  • Bubbling, peeling, cracking, colour change — covered
  • Registered in your name within 30 days of install
  • It covers the film, not the labour to redo it
  • It stays with you, not with the car when you sell it
  • I file the claim for you

The first week

New tint looks slightly cloudy while the mounting solution dries out. That's normal and it clears on its own.

  • Leave the windows up for 3–5 days
  • Haze and small water pockets fade as it cures
  • Sun speeds it up; a cold week slows it down
  • Clean with anything ammonia-free, once it's cured
  • Still seeing something after two weeks? Come back
