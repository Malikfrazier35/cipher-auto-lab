Where we fit

This is not fleet washing, and it shouldn't be priced like it.

What we do
$28–76

Per vehicle, inside included

Hand work, one van, on your site. The exterior gets done properly and — the part almost nobody sells on a schedule — so does the cab. That is where your driver spends eight hours a day and where your customer sits down.

The gap is deliberate. Every fleet operator we can find sells exterior washing and treats interiors as an occasional extra. A trade van's interior is the part that gets genuinely bad, the part a pressure washer physically cannot reach, and the part your customers notice. It is also the work a one-van operation is actually better at.

The part you can't get elsewhere

A photographed condition report on every vehicle, every visit.

Your actual problem probably isn't dirty vans. It is not knowing what state your fleet is in — which one picked up a dent, whose bumper is hanging, which cab has been lived in for three weeks.

We are already stood at every vehicle with a phone in hand. So each visit you get a dated set of photos per vehicle, plus anything worth flagging: fresh damage, a tire that looks low, a warning light on the dash, a leak under a van. It costs us nothing and it is usually the reason people renew.

Straight answers

What we won't take on.

One van and one person. Saying yes to the wrong contract means failing somebody, and it is cheaper for both of us to work that out now.

Depots of 25+ heavy trucks.

That is a pressure-rig job with a crew and water reclamation. We would be slower and dearer and you would be right to be annoyed.

Dealership lot prep at lot-prep prices.

Volume used-car prep runs $40–60 a car and is won on speed. It would eat every hour we have at a rate that makes it worse than the consumer work it displaces.

Sites with no water runoff route or no space.

We carry our own water and capture what we sensibly can, but a vehicle needs about 15 feet around it and somewhere for water to go that isn't a storm drain full of soap.

Biohazard, mould or infestation.

Different equipment, different insurance. We will tell you on sight and not charge you for the visit.

A vehicle that isn't there.

Not a refusal, just the rule: if a van is out on a job when we arrive, it rolls to the next visit at no charge. If more than half the roster is missing twice running, we will ring you and change the day rather than keep turning up to an empty lot.
