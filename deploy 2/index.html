The walk-around promise

We don't take payment until we've walked around the car together and you've told us you're happy. If something isn't right, it gets fixed while we're still standing there. Not a callback, not a next-time — on the spot.

If we damage anything, you'll hear it from us before you spot it yourself, and our insurance covers it. That's the whole reason we carry it.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do you need my water or electricity?

No. The van carries its own water tank and power, so we can work at an apartment complex or an office lot the same as a house. If a spigot is handy we'll happily use it, but nothing depends on it.

How much space do you need?

About 15 feet around the vehicle — roughly a standard driveway or two parking spaces. Covered parking is a bonus in bad weather but not required.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is usually fine for interior work. For exterior polishing or ceramic coating we'll reschedule, at no charge, to protect the result. We'll call you the morning of if the forecast turns.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Plenty of clients leave the keys and go to work. We'll send photos when we start and when we finish, and the walk-around can happen by video if you'd rather.

Why is it "starting at"?

A three-row SUV takes longer than a Civic, and a car with heavy pet hair or ground-in salt takes longer still. We quote the real number up front once we've seen photos, so the price you agree to is the price you pay.

You're new — why should I trust you with my car?

Fair question, and the honest answer is that you shouldn't take our word for it. So it's set up so you don't have to: the price is fixed before we start, you pay nothing up front, and you don't pay at all until you've inspected the car with us. Cipher Auto Lab is a registered Connecticut LLC and fully insured — ask and we'll send the certificate before you book. If we damage something, it's covered and you'll hear it from us first.

What if a stain won't come out?

Some won't. Dye transfer, old bleach spots, sun-cracked leather and ground-in salt damage are sometimes permanent, and no amount of product changes that. If it's visible going in you'll be told before the quote, and if it turns up mid-job we stop and talk to you rather than quietly charge for a result we can't deliver.

When does window tinting start?

We're targeting later this year. Mention it when you book and we'll add you to the early list — that group gets first appointments and launch pricing.
